What I do

I lead content from strategy through execution. I start by understanding what a brand needs and what its audience is asking for, then plan content that does the job. Every plan is built on established content strategy frameworks.

Content Strategy

More than a promotion plan, more than a content calendar. Content should have real, targeted impact and answer defined business goals. I’m so passionate about this that I created the Content Strategy Library.

Content Operations

A 30,000-foot view of how your written and designed marketing communications come together and accomplish your goals.

Audience and market insights

Marketing is a dialogue. So who is speaking to whom? Research and data analysis helps you know your customers and clients.

Competitor Displacement

Content is a battlefield, not a broadcast. No longer a simple matter of page rank, this means actively identifying claims and statistics and building up your E-E-A-T to rise above competitors.

Thought Leadership

In a digital ecosystem where attention and trust are the currencies, real experts with real expertise still matter. But experts aren’t always writers. I provide the connective tissue for industry experts to rise to the top of digital discourse.

Sales Enablement

The teams directly driving conversions can’t go into battle unequipped. Content gives them the tools and leverage they need to accomplish their work.

Search visibility

Search is evolving. Google’s AI mode passed 1 billion monthly users, but only 22% of marketers track AI visibility. What are AI platforms saying about your brand? And more importantly, how do you influence them?
