A tale of two audiences

Discover Atlanta, (Atlanta Convention & Visitors Bureau, or ACVB) markets the city to two distinct audiences who arrive through different entry points... and sometimes turn out to be the same person.

ClientAtlanta Convention & Visitors Bureau
RoleContent strategy, editorial
Tenure~7 years
MediaVideo courtesy ACVB

A destination marketing site was asking every visitor to sort themselves.

The copy was fine, but who was it for? If we didn't know, how would visitors know? They look to their host for guidance, after all.

The drive market and the fly market

People choosing where to spend a weekend.

Planners scouting a host city

Evaluating venues for a conference that is still two years out.

Two separate lines of business were funded through different mechanisms: hotel occupancy tax on one side, dues from member attractions and restaurants on the other.

A planner tours the city once, then hears from us for years.

They have little use for this weekend's event list after they go back home. However, it's a low buzz of what the city actually feels like when it is full of people.

Both audiences use the same website.

On their own time, a convention attendee = a tourist.

Research showed attendees extending their stay to add leisure plans, sometimes bringing family. A meeting planner has needs, too: dining, nightlife, and so on.

The answer: Realistic paths and relevant tone.

What I'd do differently today

Audience

Jobs-to-be-Done / Value Prop Canvas

Sort readers by the job they're trying to finish, not the demographic they belong to.

View on Content Strategy Library

I was thinking in terms of audiences, but audiences are demographic. “Jobs-to-be-done” are situational, and situation accounts for behavior.

They were already serving their own needs.

The convention audience isn't a distinct segment to address separately. At times, it proved to be a leisure audience with a business reason to be in town and a narrower window in which to enjoy it. The right content and proper traffic analysis caught this signal and made it actionable.
