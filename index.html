I'm Tommy, a marketer and thought leader who has spent two decades planning and executing strategic content.

Content strategy is more than a calendar, more than social promotion. It's research, problem solving and transformation. And like a good game of chess, it's thinking several moves ahead.

That instinct has carried across online retail, tourism, healthcare, and fintech, from a founding B Corporation to a national staffing platform. That's why I built the Content Strategy Library.
